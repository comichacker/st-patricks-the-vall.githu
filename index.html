Key Milestones & Legacy

Geographic & Strategic Foundation: Situated along the Asumbi–Suneka road in Rangwe Sub-County, Homa Bay County, the school benefits from Asumbi's historic legacy as an educational hub. It functions as a specialized private institution for primary and junior secondary learners.

Academic & Technological Evolution: Seamlessly transitioned from the traditional 8-4-4 system to the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), expanding infrastructure to host Junior Secondary classes (Grades 7, 8, and 9) alongside modern computer and technical learning tools.

Co-Curricular Renown: A recognized regional powerhouse, achieving top national rankings in the Kenya Music and Drama Festivals (including 3rd place nationally in French Choral Verse) alongside championship titles in regional sports and volleyball.
